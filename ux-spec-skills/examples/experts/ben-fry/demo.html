Don't skip a stage

Turning raw "Revenue pulse" exports into a trustworthy chart. The order of operations is the lesson.

Before · jump to the chart skips acquire, parse, filter, mine
Acquire → Parse → Filter → Mine → Represent

Result: a slick chart built on unvetted rows — duplicates, wrong currency, test accounts still in.

After · complete path every stage earns the chart's trust
  1. Acquire
  2. →
  3. Parse
  4. →
  5. Filter
  6. →
  7. Mine
  8. →
  9. Represent
  10. →
  11. Refine
  12. →
  13. Interact

Result: the same chart, but every number traces back to filtered, parsed, acquired source.

What to notice: the crossed-out stages in the top row are exactly where trust is won or lost — acquire, parse, filter, and mine happen before "represent," not after.
