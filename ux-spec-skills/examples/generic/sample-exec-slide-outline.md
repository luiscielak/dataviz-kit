# Sample: Executive slide outline — Q3 Revenue review

**Audience**: C-suite, Revenue leadership  
**Desired outcome**: Approve reallocation of spend to West and Product B  
**Last updated**: 2025-02-03  

---

## Purpose & outcome

- Summarize Q3 revenue performance vs plan and prior year.
- Explain regional and product variance; highlight West underperformance and Product B overperformance.
- **Outcome**: Approval to reallocate marketing and sales spend to West and Product B for Q4.

---

## Narrative arc

- **Opening**: Q3 revenue on plan overall; two moves matter for Q4.
- **Evidence**: Plan vs actual by region and product; West and Product B callouts; trend from weekly dashboard.
- **Conclusion**: West underinvested; Product B demand exceeds capacity; reallocation is low-risk, high-impact.
- **Ask**: Approve reallocation; confirm targets for West and Product B by next week.

---

## Slide outline

| Slide # | Title | Purpose | Key content | Visual |

| 1 | Q3 Revenue — on plan, two moves for Q4 | Hook | One sentence: on plan; West and Product B are the story | Title + 1 line |
| 2 | Revenue vs plan and YoY | Context | Q3 actual vs plan vs prior year; % to plan | Bar or single comparison |
| 3 | By region — West below plan | Evidence | Revenue by region; West highlighted; variance % | Bar chart, West called out |

| 8 | Appendix — weekly trend | Backup | 12-week revenue trend (from dashboard) | Line chart |
