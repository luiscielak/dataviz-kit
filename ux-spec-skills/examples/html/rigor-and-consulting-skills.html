Metric definitions

Hover the ⓘ to see the tooltip shown inline in the live dashboard.

Metric Definition Source / refresh
Bookings ⓘ New + expansion revenue closed in the period. Excludes renewals under $10k. As of 2025-02-03 06:00 UTC. New + expansion revenue closed in the period Salesforce · nightly 02:00 UTC
MRR ⓘ sum(ARR) / 12. Billing system, daily 04:00 UTC. Currency USD-normalized at sync time. Recurring revenue, monthly rate, as of period end Billing system · daily 04:00 UTC
NRR ⓘ (start ARR + expansion − contraction − churn) / start ARR. Cohort defined at contract start, not calendar year. Net revenue retention vs. same cohort last period Billing system · daily 04:00 UTC

Freshness banner shown dashboard-wide when no successful sync in 36h. See full lineage and caveats in the spec.
