# Alberto Cairo

**Tier:** Practice
**Why in this kit:** Truthfulness paired with visual form—bridges academic rigor and day-to-day practice.

## Bio

Alberto Cairo is a journalist and educator who holds the Knight Chair in Visual Journalism at the University of Miami. His books connect the ethics of honest charts with the craft of making them, which is why he sits between the kit's rigor checklists and its practical skills.

In *The Truthful Art* he frames five qualities of a good visualization—truthful, functional, beautiful, insightful, and enlightening—while *How Charts Lie* catalogs the everyday ways charts mislead, from truncated axes to missing base rates. His emphasis is that integrity is a design decision, not an afterthought.

For this kit, Cairo is the truthfulness lens: does the visual form let the chart claim only what the data honestly supports?
