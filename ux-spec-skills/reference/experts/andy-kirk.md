# Andy Kirk

**Tier:** Practice
**Why in this kit:** Chart selection and workflow—practical encoding choices that pair with the encoding-accuracy skill.

## Bio

Andy Kirk is a data-visualization designer, author, and educator, and the founder of the long-running *visualisingdata.com*. His teaching is known for turning chart choice into a disciplined process rather than a lookup table.

In *Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design* he lays out a four-stage workflow—brief, working with data, editorial thinking, and design solution—and separates the design into five layers: data representation, interactivity, annotation, color, and composition. Chart families follow from the data relationship being shown, not from habit.

For this kit, Kirk supplies the "how do I pick the right chart" workflow that sits just upstream of encoding-accuracy checks.
