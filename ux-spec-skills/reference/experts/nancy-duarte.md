# Nancy Duarte

**Tier:** Practice
**Why in this kit:** Executive narrative and slide structure—pairs with Minto and the exec slide outline skill.

## Bio

Nancy Duarte is a communication expert whose firm has designed presentations for many of the world's best-known talks and launches. Her books turned presentation craft into a repeatable method rather than a matter of taste.

In *Resonate* she describes persuasive talks as a structured movement between "what is" and "what could be," building tension that resolves in a call to action, with a memorable STAR moment as an anchor. *slide:ology* covers the visual craft: one idea per slide, action titles, and disciplined use of type and imagery.

For this kit, Duarte governs the emotional and contrast arc of executive storytelling—complementing Minto's answer-first logic with a narrative that moves an audience to act.
