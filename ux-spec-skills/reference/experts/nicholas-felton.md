# Nicholas Felton

**Tier:** Practice
**Why in this kit:** Editorial information design—dense typographic layouts and small multiples as narrative.

## Bio

Nicholas Felton is an information designer known for the *Feltron Annual Reports*, a series of personal yearbooks that turned a year of mundane data into carefully edited, magazine-quality reports. The work made "personal data as narrative" a recognizable design genre.

He co-created Daytum, a personal data-collection service, and later worked on Facebook's Timeline, carrying editorial data design into mainstream products. His reports are studied for how they hold enormous density without becoming noise: strict grids, a disciplined type scale, and small multiples that invite comparison.

For this kit, Felton stands for the craft of the reference or annual artifact—when the job is to condense many facts into one coherent, browsable page rather than a live monitoring surface.

## Known works

- *Feltron Annual Reports* (2005–2014) — personal-data annual reports
- Daytum (2008) — personal data collection and reporting service
- Facebook Timeline — data-design contributions
